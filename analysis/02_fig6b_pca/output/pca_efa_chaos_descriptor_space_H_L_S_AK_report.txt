3. Excluded variables
   EFA was not used as a PCA input; it was used only for percentile coloring and post-PCA correlation.
   Related target columns were not used.
   Configurational entropy columns M-R were not used because the requested feature blocks are H-L and S-AK.

4. Preprocessing
   All selected features were converted to numeric values. Missing feature values were median-imputed.
   Features were standardized to zero mean and unit variance before PCA.
   PC2 was oriented to have a positive Spearman correlation with EFA percentile; PCA signs are arbitrary.

8. Interpretation
   The PC1-PC2 projection combines DFT lattice distortion and chemistry descriptors in one latent CHAOS descriptor space.
   A systematic EFA gradient in this plane indicates that high-EFA systems are organized by the descriptor space, not by EFA target leakage.
   The loading plots identify whether the EFA-sensitive latent direction is dominated by structural distortion metrics, chemistry-only metrics, or both.
