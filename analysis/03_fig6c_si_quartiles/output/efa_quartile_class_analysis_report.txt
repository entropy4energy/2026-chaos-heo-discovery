EFA quartile-class analysis: calculation report
========================================================================

1. Data source
   Input workbook: analysis/03_fig6c_si_quartiles/input/locked_493.xlsx
   Sheet: combined
   Raw rows in sheet: 493
   Rows used after dropping missing/non-numeric EFA: 493

2. Quartile definitions
   EFA quartile: Q1 = low EFA, Q2 = intermediate-low EFA, Q3 = intermediate-high EFA, Q4 = high EFA.
   Quartiles are assigned using percentile ranks scaled to [0, 1].

3. Descriptors compared across EFA quartiles
   lat_distortion_misfit_mean -> $\bar{\epsilon}$
   lat_distortion_relax_distance_mean -> $\bar{d}$
   VEC -> VEC
   delta_radii_Ghosh08 -> $\delta r_{\mathrm{Ghosh}}$
   param_geo_radii_Ghosh08 -> $\Lambda_{\mathrm{Ghosh}}$
   delta_electronegativity_Ghosh -> $\delta\chi_{\mathrm{Ghosh}}$

4. Key EFA-quartile trends: raw means from Q1 to Q4
   $\bar{\epsilon}$: Q1 mean = 0.186567; Q4 mean = 0.0968177; Spearman trend vs quartile = -0.424
   $\bar{d}$: Q1 mean = 0.325454; Q4 mean = 0.176789; Spearman trend vs quartile = -0.348
   VEC: Q1 mean = 5.82724; Q4 mean = 6.73286; Spearman trend vs quartile = 0.398
   $\delta r_{\mathrm{Ghosh}}$: Q1 mean = 18.5342; Q4 mean = 12.2221; Spearman trend vs quartile = -0.325
   $\Lambda_{\mathrm{Ghosh}}$: Q1 mean = 0.00525179; Q4 mean = 0.0220931; Spearman trend vs quartile = 0.325
   $\delta\chi_{\mathrm{Ghosh}}$: Q1 mean = 0.49848; Q4 mean = 0.366724; Spearman trend vs quartile = -0.210

5. Statistical tests
   For each descriptor, the Excel workbook reports Spearman correlation against quartile index,
   Kruskal-Wallis tests across Q1-Q4, and Mann-Whitney U tests comparing Q1 and Q4.
   These tests are intended as trend diagnostics; the manuscript figure should emphasize
   percentile-ranked distributions to avoid exposing system-specific raw values.

6. Output files
   Main Excel workbook: analysis/03_fig6c_si_quartiles/output/efa_quartile_class_analysis.xlsx
   OriginPro subplot Excel workbook: analysis/03_fig6c_si_quartiles/output/efa_quartile_class_originpro_subplots.xlsx
   TXT report: analysis/03_fig6c_si_quartiles/output/efa_quartile_class_analysis_report.txt

7. Suggested manuscript interpretation
   High-EFA quartiles show lower lattice-distortion metrics, lower Ghosh radius mismatch,
   and higher Λ_Ghosh/VEC compared with low-EFA quartiles. These class-based distributions
   provide a ranking while hiding individual EFA values.
